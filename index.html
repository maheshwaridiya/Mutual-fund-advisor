Verified Mutual Fund & Equity Taxation Guide

Reflects current capital gains rules effective after the July 23, 2024 Union Budget & Finance Act 2025/2026.

≥ 65% Domestic Equity

Equity Funds & Listed Shares

Short-Term (Held ≤ 12M): 20% under Sec 111A (+ 4% cess).
Long-Term (Held > 12M): 12.5% on gains exceeding ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year (Sec 112A, no indexation).

Section 80C Eligible

ELSS Tax Saving Funds

Lock-in Period: Mandatory 3 Years.
Tax Deduction: Up to ₹1.5 Lakh/yr under Sec 80C (Old Tax Regime).
Redemption Tax: LTCG at 12.5% above ₹1.25 Lakh annual exemption.

< 35% Equity (Sec 50AA)

Specified Debt Mutual Funds

Units bought on/after Apr 1, 2023: All capital gains (regardless of holding period) are added to income and taxed at your Applicable Income Tax Slab Rate without indexation.

Global / FoF / Gold

International & Gold Funds

Short-Term (Held ≤ 24M): Taxed at Income Tax Slab Rate.
Long-Term (Held > 24M): Taxed at flat 12.5% LTCG without indexation (post April 1, 2025 rules).
